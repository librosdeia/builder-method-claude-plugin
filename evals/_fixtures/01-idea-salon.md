# booking-app — Idea

## What it is
A small website where the clients of a one-person hair salon book appointments online.

## What it is for
To stop booking by phone and WhatsApp, and to avoid double bookings.

## Who it is for
The salon's clients, and the hairdresser who owns it.

## How it is used
Clients pick a service, see the free slots of the week and book with name and phone. They can cancel up to 24 h before. Each morning the owner checks the day's agenda on her phone.

## Not in v1
- Online payments
- Client accounts and passwords
