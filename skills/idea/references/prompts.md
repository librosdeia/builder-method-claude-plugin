# Builder Method — original Idea prompts (English)

These are the prompts of the method's course, translated. The skill follows their intent; the user never has to type them.

## To start
> I want to solve this problem and I have all this information. Help me go deeper into the topic, look at possible approaches and broaden my knowledge so I can pin down the idea I should build. Think it through, ask me questions and help me make the idea concrete. Don't implement anything — I only want you to help me develop the idea.

## To close
> Describe the product idea in simple terms, without adding conclusions: what it is, what it is for, who it is for, and interesting sources. Don't draw conclusions of your own.
