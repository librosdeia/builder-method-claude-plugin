# DNS

## What it is
The internet's phone book. It translates a name like `yoursalon.com` into the address of the computer where your app lives.

## Why it matters
To use your own domain, you add a few DNS records at the company where you bought it, pointing to your host.

## Example
A CNAME record `www → your-app.up.railway.app` sends visitors of www.yoursalon.com to your app.
